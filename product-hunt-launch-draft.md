# Monet — Product Hunt launch draft

## Product name

Monet

## Tagline

Never guess which card to use.

## One-line description

Monet tells you which credit card earns the most for every purchase.

## Product Hunt description

Your best rewards card changes with every purchase. Monet makes the right choice obvious.

Add the cards you carry to your Monet wallet, search for any merchant, and get an instant recommendation for which card to use. No spreadsheets, no memorizing bonus categories, and no card numbers or account details required.

Monet is built for people who want to earn more rewards without turning every checkout into a research project.

### How it works

1. Choose the cards in your wallet.
2. Search for a merchant or category.
3. Use the card Monet recommends.

Try the web demo at https://tapmonet.com/app

## Maker comment

Hi Product Hunt 👋

I built Monet because rewards cards are powerful but exhausting to keep track of. The card that earns most at a restaurant may not be the same one you want at a grocery store, coffee shop, or streaming service—and most of us don’t want to maintain a spreadsheet just to make a quick purchase.

Monet turns that decision into a simple search. Add the cards you already carry, look up where you’re shopping, and get a clear recommendation before you pay.

Monet does not need your card numbers or bank credentials. It’s designed to help you use the wallet you already have with a little less mental math.

If you try it, I’d love to know:

- Which merchants or categories you want Monet to support next
- Whether the recommendation is clear enough at checkout speed
- What would make you trust Monet with your everyday wallet

Thanks for checking it out!

Media QA checklist:

- Keep the product UI large enough to read on a phone-sized preview.
- Do not crop the top/bottom of mobile screenshots unless the crop is intentional and tested.
- Export a separate landscape hero image instead of reusing the portrait app capture.
- Avoid placing small text in the image; keep the product promise in the listing copy when possible.
- Check light and dark mode separately before exporting.
- Verify the final files in the Product Hunt upload preview, not just locally.
