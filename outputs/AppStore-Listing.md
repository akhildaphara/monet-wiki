## Description (4000 char max)

Never guess which card to use again.

Monet is the simplest way to maximize your credit card rewards. Before you pay, search the merchant — Monet instantly tells you which card in YOUR wallet earns the most. No spreadsheets, no memorizing rotating categories, no guesswork.

WHY MONET

• Instant answers — Search any store or category and get the best card from your wallet in a tap.
• Your real wallet — Build your wallet from a curated, growing catalog of cards. Monet tracks the details: rotating quarterly categories, tiered bonuses, and category multipliers.
• See what you miss — Connect your accounts securely through Plaid and Monet cross-references every transaction with your wallet, showing what you actually earned vs. what the optimal card would have earned.
• Works offline — Core recommendations work even without a connection.
• Private by design — We never sell your data. Bank connections are read-only and powered by Plaid. Disconnect anytime.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Wallet — Add the cards you carry.
2. Spend — Search the merchant before you pay; Monet names the best card.
3. Insights — Optionally connect accounts to see missed rewards over time.

Try it free, no account required — explore as a guest, and sign in with Apple or Google whenever you want to sync across devices.

Monet is a decision-support tool, not financial advice. Reward rates are based on publicly available information; always verify with your card issuer.

Questions or feedback? feedback@monet.app

## What to Test (TestFlight — "Test Information" field)

Welcome to the Monet beta! Thanks for helping shape v1. Please try:

1. GETTING STARTED
   - Continue as guest (no account), OR sign in with Apple / Google.
   - Add 3–5 of your credit cards to your wallet.

2. CORE FLOW — does it pick the right card?
   - Search merchants you actually shop at (Whole Foods, Starbucks, Amazon, a local restaurant).
   - Search by category when offline (turn on Airplane Mode).
   - Tell us if any recommendation looks WRONG — that's the most valuable feedback.

3. INSIGHTS (You can skip this for now)

4. ACCOUNTS
   - Sign out and back in — does your wallet persist?

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Card catalog is not huge; tell us which card you wish was supported.
- Bilt Card management coming soon.

REPORT ISSUES
- In-app: Profile → Send Feedback.
- Please include: what you searched, what it recommended, what you expected.
