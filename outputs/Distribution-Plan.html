Parallel Track — Company Registration & Affiliate Setup

Weeks 1–6 (Concurrent)
Prerequisite: Apple requires a legal entity with a D-U-N-S number for an Organization Developer account. Registering a company also enables application to credit card affiliate networks, creating a native referral revenue stream from Monet's market-best card recommendations.

1. Company Registration & Apple Developer Organization

Step Description Estimated Duration
LLC Formation & EIN Form an LLC (Delaware or home state). Obtain federal EIN from IRS. 1–2 weeks
D-U-N-S Number Request free D-U-N-S identifier from Dun & Bradstreet for the new company. 1–2 weeks
Apple Organization Enrollment Transition Apple Developer account to Organization status using D-U-N-S number. 1–2 weeks
App Store Connect Verification Confirm bundle ID akhil.Monet and capabilities under the Organization entity. 1 day

2. Credit Card Affiliate Program Setup

Component Details
Affiliate Networks Apply to Commission Junction (CJ), Impact.com, and Rakuten Advertising as the registered entity for card issuer programs (Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi).
In-App Referral Links When Monet highlights a market-best card that the user does not currently hold, include an affiliate referral link.
Compliance Disclosure Include explicit disclosure on the website and app: "We may receive compensation when you apply through our links. This does not impact our ranking algorithms."

Launch across organic channels in sequence. Build initial user base and validate retention before spending any ad dollars.
