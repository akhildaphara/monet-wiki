TestFlight Compliance Checklist

To ensure the Monet app passes Apple's rigorous App Store and TestFlight review processes, particularly under Guideline 5.1.1 (Privacy) and Guideline 5.2.1 (Intellectual Property), we maintain a strict pre-launch checklist. This document serves as the central record for tracking necessary compliance work, including privacy policy integration, bank card trademark safety, and user account deletion requirements.

Pre-Launch Requirements Checklist

1. In-App User Account Deletion (CRITICAL)

  • [ ] Add a prominent "Delete Account" button in ProfileView.swift.
  • [ ] Implement the corresponding data-wipe backend endpoint in croe to erase all user transaction records, Plaid credentials, and overrides from DynamoDB.
  • Why: Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) mandates that any app supporting account creation must also offer user-initiated in-app account deletion.

2. Privacy Policy & Financial Data

  • [x] Host a secure and comprehensive Privacy Policy on the website.
  • [ ] Add a link to the Privacy Policy inside the iOS app UI (e.g., in Settings or Profile under Support).
  • Why: Apple subjects financial aggregators linking to personal banking info (via Plaid) to deep data-minimization audits.

3. Apple Developer Organization Account

  • [ ] Transition or register the developer portal credentials under an Organization account.
  • Why: Apple rarely approves financial apps or third-party card optimization tools submitted by Individual developer accounts.
  • Details: See the guide on Apple-Developer-Org-Registration.

4. Trademark & Nominative Fair Use

  • [x] Utilize procedural gradients and faux EMV chips for card assets (PreviewCardView.swift, MiniCardView.swift) instead of bank logo assets.
  • [x] Keep bank card text representation (e.g., "Chase Sapphire Reserve") strictly textual and compliant under Nominative Fair Use.
